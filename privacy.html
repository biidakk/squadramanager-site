Squadra Manager – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Squadra Manager does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Your saved games

Your careers are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them, unless you exported a save file yourself.

Purchases

If you buy the full game, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details.

Files you import

Name or database files you import stay on your device and are only used inside the game.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: support@squadramanager.com
