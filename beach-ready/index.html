Beach Ready – Support

Beach Ready: Workout Planner helps you follow a gym or home workout plan step by step: ready-made plans, a rest timer, animated exercise demos and your progress. No account, no ads, and your data stays on your phone.

Contact us

Questions, problems or ideas? Email contact@calmshops.co.uk and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. It helps to tell us your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Just open the app, pick a plan and start.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. We never receive your workouts. See our privacy policy.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Your data can come with you if your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) is switched on and you restore that backup on the new phone. Beach Ready has no online account, so there is no other way to sync.

How do I switch between kg and lb?

Open Settings in the app and choose your unit.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all workout data. Deleting the app also removes everything.

How does the app suggest my next weight?

After each exercise you tell the app whether you could have done more. It uses that and what you lifted to suggest the next weight and reps. These are only suggestions – always listen to your body.

Health and safety

Beach Ready is a workout planner, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or have not exercised for a long time, check with your doctor first. Stop straight away if you feel pain, dizziness or chest pain.
