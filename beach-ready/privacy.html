Beach Ready – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The short version

Everything you enter in Beach Ready: Workout Planner stays on your phone. The app has no account, no sign-in, no adverts and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your data.

Who we are

Beach Ready is made by Calmshops LTD, United Kingdom.

What is stored, and where

Your plans, workouts, sets, weights, reps, cardio times and distances, your answers such as “could you have done more?”, and your settings are saved in a small database inside the app on your phone.

This data is never sent to us or anyone else. The app does not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and it does not need an internet connection to work.

Your data may be included in your phone’s own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) if you have that switched on. That is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Health information

Workout logs can count as health and fitness information. Because it never leaves your phone, only you can see it.

App stores

When you download Beach Ready, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) may collect information about the download under their own privacy policies. We may see anonymous, combined statistics from them, such as the number of downloads, but nothing that identifies you or shows your workouts.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your workout history at any time in the app under Settings → Delete all workout data. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

Beach Ready is meant for adults and is not aimed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
