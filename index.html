Questions

Is it free?

Yes, the beta is free and there are no ads. If a paid full version comes later, players who joined early keep everything they have.

Does it need the internet?

No. The whole game plays offline on your phone.

Which countries and leagues are in?

44 countries have their own leagues and cups. England, Germany, Spain, Italy, France, Portugal, the Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Poland, Czechia, Scotland, Turkey, Brazil and Argentina go deep down the pyramid, so you can start in a regional league and climb.

Are the clubs and players real?

The game comes with its own club and player names. With the editor you can rename and edit everything, export it as a database file and load it again in a new career.

Can I move my save to a new phone?

Yes: Export saves your career as a file, and Import loads it on the other phone.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Czech, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Turkish.
