About the game

Squadra Manager is a football manager you can play in minutes, not hours. Inspired by the quick, simple managers of the early 2000s, it lets you take over a club in one of more than 20 countries and run everything that matters: live 2D matches with substitutions and tactics on the fly, transfers where rich clubs from the big leagues pay more for your stars, a youth academy, national cups, three European competitions with moving coefficients, and national teams with their real World Cup and Euro history. An editor career lets players edit clubs and players and share their own databases.

Key features

  • A whole season in minutes: quick screens, big decisions
  • Live 2D matches with commentary, subs and tactics
  • Transfers, loans and contracts; bids depend on the buyer's league
  • Youth academy and U-19 league
  • Cups, three continental competitions, UEFA-style coefficients
  • National teams: real history, all-time scorers, the national job
  • Stadium, facilities and finances that grow with the club
  • Editor career and shareable database files
  • No ads, no energy bars, plays offline
