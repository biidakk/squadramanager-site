4.2.1

25 October 2026
  • Czech: the scenario difficulties read Snadné, Těžké and Velmi těžké.

4.2

25 October 2026
  • The home screen always shows eight tiles: your club, the U-19 league and the top scorers fill any gaps.
  • U-19 league with every club's real teenagers: goals, assists, a Scorers tab and news of standout youngsters.
  • Youngsters who have not played for the first team cost a training compensation.
  • News and fan posts follow the language you play in, also after switching.

4.1.2

24 October 2026
  • Fairer money: every club starts with a budget that matches its wages, in every country. Small clubs in the local leagues have to earn their way up.
  • Database files can give the rich clubs of each league more money than the rest.
  • Busier transfer market between computer clubs.

24 October 2026
  • Phones: bigger names, shirt numbers and ratings on the pitch.
  • A tidier home screen: all your competitions in two rows, the next matches and the full schedule closer together.
  • New Career fits every language on small screens.
